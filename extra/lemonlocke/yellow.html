Lemonlocke Yellow

Text wall:

rival 1A (the one on route 22)- see below for the pokemon to catch. you're gonna wanna beat this guy since jolteon is the easiest of the eeveelutions to wall (but maybe it doesn't matter that much).
brock- get a L4 rattata on route 1, a nidoran (male) from route 2, a spearow from route 22, and get the 1% pidgeotto from viridian forest. lead pikachu, growl as much as possible, then tail whip with rattata as much as possible. finish it off with pidgeotto. use the remaining pokemon to growl and leer the onix as much as possible so that pidgeotto can finish it off. be wary of bind because it can do lots of damage.
misty- get the bulbasaur from cerulean city. leech seed plus growl and pidgeotto are the key to victory. the only new additions to the party would be the spearow before mt moon, the geodude in mt moon (which can be left in pc), and another pidgeotto from route 24. misty only has 2 pokemon so its pretty likely that leech seed will go off against both pokemon, which will support the rest of the team.
surge- catch a dugtrio from diglett cave. click dig.
erika- get a eevee -> flareon from celadon, get a kadabra from route 8, and lead flareon. use tail whip a few times against tangela since it only has weak moves to hit you, spam quick attack until it goes down (switch if you get binded). get weepinbell down so that kadabra can finish it off without taking a hit. finish gloom off with either kadabra or dugtrio if needed.
rival 4 lavender town- lead bulbasaur and leech seed the fearow until death, do the same for shellder with growl too. use flareon to quick attack the vulpix, the sandshrew, and use kadabra and dugtrio to just clean up.
koga- catch a tentacruel on route 19, get a paras/parasect from the safari zone with spore, get the snorlax from route 12. use surf with tentacruel as much as you can against venonat, use the dugtrio and kadabra to finish off the rest of the venonats if needed. sacrifice bulbasaur or something to the venomoth so it doesn't double team. send in parasect to spore (the venomoth will only use toxic or leech life which is terrible), then switch in snorlax to finish off venomoth (should be fine even if it wakes up, but you can send in parasect again if needed to reset the situation.
rival 5 silph co- get zapdos with some help from parasect's spore. zapdos solos, make sure to teach it thunderbolt.
giovanni silph co- zapdos solos but might need help from tentacruel.
sabrina- teach zapdos bide/whirlwind or some other non-damaging move and swift. get to -6 accuracy so that you can get badge boosts in attack and speed. swift should ohko abra, kadabra, and should be a 2hko on alakazam. if somehow alakazam wins, use articuno and give sabrina a bouquet of roses if she manages to take articuno out too.
blaine- zapdos solos, if not articuno cleans up since fire doesn't resist ice.
giovanni- articuno solos usually.
rival 6- lead articuno and spam ice beam, switch to zapdos on the cloyster and take out it and the jolteon because jolteon only uses agility.

lorelei- use thunderbolt zapdos against the water types, sacrifice something to jynx to let articuno fly into jynx
bruno- ice beam the onix, drill peck the fighters with zapdos
agatha- use graveler to earthquake her pokemon, and use zapdos as backup cause this battle is always going to be rng dependent. even still, this is a very hard battle to lose.
lance- lead zapdos and thunderbolt the gyarados, take out the first dragonair with graveler, then take out the second with articuno. take out the remaining two with articuno.
champ- use ice beam on the sandslash, take out alakazam with articuno and zapdos, take out the fire/water/grass core with zapdos, and then use graveler for the jolteon.
